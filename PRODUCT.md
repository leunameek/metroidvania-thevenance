## Platform

Windows PC, Unity 6000.3.21f1, URP 17.3, Input System 1.20, uGUI 2.0 and TextMeshPro.

## Players and purpose

Players aged 16+ explore a narrative action adventure inspired by Muisca cosmology. The current slice teaches object inspection, a reactive turn-based duel and travel between the plaza and two world thresholds. Hands examine objects; voice can select combat actions. Mouse and keyboard remain available.

## Constraints

Preserve characters, rigs, animation, story, geometry and working mechanics. UI must distinguish historical validation from artistic interpretation. No archaeological claims may be inferred from the fictional practice objects. No mandatory camera, microphone, internet or account.

## Acceptance

Readable 720p–1440p and 16:10 UI, keyboard navigation, persistent settings, real device feedback, safe pause, adjustable text and motion. Validate screens in Unity and report hardware-dependent checks separately.

## Existing scope

Three inspection lessons, health, training combat, objectives, portals and audio exist. Three persistent checkpoint slots now restore completed lessons, training, visited worlds and played time at Plaza Núñez. The inventory records completed inspections as collected discoveries. Consumable resources and an authored dialogue sequence do not exist; UI must not invent their mechanics.
